## 💻 Projects

### [A Multilingual Framework for Dysarthria: Detection, Severity Classification, Speech-to-Text, and Clean Speech Generation](https://github.com/sofiebudman/Dysarthria_App)
A unified, multilingual AI framework for dysarthria, a speech disorder linked with neurological diseases. The system combines detection, severity classification, speech restoration, automatic speech recognition, emotion recognition, and voice cloning in one pipeline. It achieves up to 97% accuracy across English, Russian, and German, demonstrating strong cross-lingual generalization and transfer learning, with the goal of enabling accessible diagnosis and removing communication barriers.

📄 [Research Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.03986) | 🖼️ [Poster Presentation](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1nglvy9hKWfx1-oyU5b5ZshGvEtCWDWoK/edit?usp=sharing)

### [BridgeMate](https://github.com/sofiebudman/BridgeMate)
A Flask application with an AI-powered chat interface, speech-to-text, and text-to-speech, specializing in US immigration policy. BridgeMate helps users prepare for interviews by simulating real-world scenarios, offering personalized guidance, and generating step-by-step immigration roadmaps. It combines AI, automatic speech recognition, and RAG to make the immigration process clearer and more manageable.

### [MIT Beaver Works Summer Institute Projects](https://github.com/sofiebudman/Beaverworks)
Machine learning notebooks created during the MIT Beaver Works Summer Institute:

### [Epidemic Simulator](https://github.com/sofiebudman/AP_CS_Final)
A Java-based graphical simulation that models the spread of a custom-designed virus through a population. Users can visualize how infections propagate over time using configurable parameters such as infection rate, recovery rate, and population behavior.

### [Upregulation of Skint3 and Skint9 in Spaceflight Mice Reveals Adaptive Skin–Immune Responses to Microgravity](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1KoxNZUUX_kMIUTPH7V2jY2ziV-rxsUK7/edit?usp=sharing)
Analyzed the NASA OSD-254 mouse skin dataset to study microgravity's effects on skin-associated immune genes. Differential expression revealed strong upregulation of Skint3 and Skint9, and pathway enrichment showed activation of epidermal and immune response pathways. These results suggest coordinated skin–immune adaptation in microgravity and point to potential targets for supporting astronaut skin health on long-duration missions.

### [TMEM62 Gene Project](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1-E_rC3lhdT8ikUWDo4WPj6pgTEBWhmFI/view?usp=sharing)
Explores the function and significance of Transmembrane Protein 62 (TMEM62) using NCBI, UniProt, and Ensembl to analyze gene expression, protein structure, and evolutionary conservation, and to uncover potential roles in cellular processes and disease.
